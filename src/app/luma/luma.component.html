Luma Process

Luma Process Details (English)

Step 1: Research

Problem:
- 1 in 5 young people struggle with mental health issues.
- 70% of mental health issues start in adolescence.

Emotional impact:
- persistent sadness, anxiety, low self-worth
- social withdrawal from activities & relationships

Practical impact:
- sleep disruption, declining school/work performance
- relationship conflicts, potential risk behaviors

Our solution:
Luma, a mental health companion app for young people, providing a safe space to explore emotions and understand them.

Step 2: User Interviews & Personas

Based on the User Interviews, we created a Persona to always have the user in mind during the design process.
We also created a User Journey to better understand the different touchpoints of the user with the app and to identify potential pain points.

Step 3: Moodboard

We wanted our app to have a calming and supportive atmosphere.

Step 4: Prototyping

We created Wireframes for our UserTestings.

Take aways:
- Users want a more personalized experience with customizable features
- Users want a clear differentiation between the own notes and the AI-generated insights

Our solutions:
- personalized breathing exercises based on the user's current emotional state
- "Lui's Notes" and "Diary" sections to clearly separate AI insights and personal reflections
Based on the Wireframes, we created a high-fidelity prototype in Figma.
